# X draft

Any six-partite intersecting Ryser counterexample needs at least 24 edges, up from 22. We excluded 22 and 23 edges using degree constraints, overlap counts, and exact certificates. The rank-six conjecture remains open.

At 24 edges every vertex has degree at most nine. We also ruled out order-six rotations through 43 edges and any extension that keeps at least 12 unchanged rows of the affine plane of order five. The paper includes replays for both construction checks.
